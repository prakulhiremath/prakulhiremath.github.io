Selected work

Research on selection bias, sequential detection, evaluation, and uncertainty.

NeurIPS 2026

Poster · Sole author

Latency-Conditioned Selection Bias in RLHF

Discarding the slowest rollouts in asynchronous RLHF filters out long reasoning trajectories and biases training. Includes a diagnostic (EGI) and an inverse-probability correction (SPW).

Result: GSM8K accuracy with LLaMA-3-8B falls from 68.4% to 44.1% under the bias; the correction recovers 66.9%.

NeurIPS poster · Project page · OpenReview

Poster · With P. M. Bagawan, S. Bhekane

When Does Sequential Detection Collapse to a Scalar? A Necessary and Sufficient Characterisation

Gives an exact condition (EDS) for when a Bayesian sequential detection problem can be reduced to a scalar statistic without losing Bayes-optimal decisions, and the unavoidable loss when it cannot.

Result: predicted and observed lead times agree within 0.4 steps (MAE) across 72 synthetic configurations.
